Portable wrappers preserve circuits, frozen coefficients, graph sets, trajectories and statistics. study.py contains only the original load_training function plus plotting imports. Renderers omit historical review/manuscript-edit operations. Original simulation and fitting implementations are retained in original_code for provenance; use the documented root entry points instead. The historical A_joint_candidate model has the same positive-degree coefficients as U1; its fallback policy is NOT used by the probability rerunner.

`portable_optimizer.py` keeps the archived optimizer loop but supplies graphs/current formula references through packaged inputs instead of hard-coded historical directories. Refinement reruns use archived starts and the original Lightning/adjoint implementation. Historical full-pool held-out result archives are omitted; the portable pipeline uses the self-contained selected-graph manifests and paired records.
